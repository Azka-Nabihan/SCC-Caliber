# 5. Glossary of Industrial Standards, Metrics & Algorithmic Acronyms

To ensure flawless communication during executive presentations and technical Q&A, the key standards and technical terminology are defined below:

*   **ANSI/ISA-18.2 / EEMUA 191**: International management standards for industrial alarm systems, establishing that manageable alarm rates must not exceed 1 alarm per 10 minutes per console operator under steady-state conditions.
*   **Aff-F1 (Affiliation-Based F1-Score)**: A rigorous time-series evaluation metric that measures the duration and temporal affiliation between predicted and true anomaly intervals, penalizing fragmentation without the distortions of point adjustment (`arXiv:2109.05257`).
*   **COT (Coil Outlet Temperature)**: The critical temperature ($820^\circ\text{C}–$850^\circ\text{C}$) of hydrocarbon gases exiting the radiant coils of a pyrolysis steam cracker, governing ethylene/propylene selectivity and coke formation rates.
*   **DEXPI (Data Exchange in the Process Industry)**: The international XML/graph specification standard for vendor-neutral digital exchange of P&ID engineering schematics.
*   **ICNN-MPC (Input Convex Neural Network MPC)**: A model predictive control architecture where neural network weights are constrained ($W \ge 0$) to guarantee global convexity, transforming non-convex NMPC into an explicit, fast-solving convex Quadratic Program (`arXiv:2408.06580`).
*   **IEC 62443**: The global cybersecurity standard for Industrial Automation and Control Systems (IACS), defining Security Levels (SL-1 to SL-4) and zone-conduit segmentation.
*   **ISA-95 / Purdue Enterprise Reference Architecture (PERA)**: The foundational hierarchy separating physical processes (Levels 0–2), manufacturing operations management (Level 3), DMZ boundaries (Level 3.5), and enterprise business systems (Level 4).
*   **KKT (Karush–Kuhn–Tucker) Projection**: Mathematical projection layers enforcing hard linear and nonlinear equality/inequality constraints directly into deep neural network inference (`arXiv:2606.10682`, `arXiv:2402.07251`).
*   **LOTO (Lockout / Tagout)**: Critical process safety procedure used to ensure that equipment and piping lines are isolated, depressurized, and de-energized before maintenance or turnaround work.
*   **MOC (Management of Change)**: Regulatory Process Safety Management (OSHA 1910.119 / CCPS) requirement mandating exhaustive engineering reviews (including HAZOP) before any plant hardware or operational setpoint modification.
*   **PICNN (Parametric Input Convex Neural Network)**: A neural architecture utilized as a Control Barrier Function (CBF) to project unconstrained reinforcement learning actions onto a strictly safe operational envelope (`arXiv:2507.22640`).
*   **PINN (Physics-Informed Neural Network)**: Neural network architectures that embed differential conservation laws (mass, momentum, energy, chemical kinetics) into the training loss and model topology.
*   **VLE (Vapor-Liquid Equilibrium)**: Thermodynamic phase equilibrium governing distillation separation efficiency, modeled dynamically via Raoult's, Wilson, or NRTL equations.
*   **VUS-PR (Volume Under Surface of Precision-Recall)**: Generalized time-series anomaly detection metric that assesses accuracy across varying temporal buffer windows and detection thresholds (`arXiv:2206.13167`).
